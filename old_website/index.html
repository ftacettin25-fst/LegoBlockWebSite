FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our custom BrickHeadz

Our design process typically takes 5–7 business days from the moment you submit your photo. Complex designs with special accessories may take an additional 2–3 days. You'll receive a digital preview for approval before production begins.
Clear, well-lit front-facing photos work best. We recommend portrait-style photos where facial features and clothing are clearly visible. You can also send multiple photos from different angles for the most accurate result.
Each custom BrickHeadz kit includes all the LEGO-compatible bricks needed to build your figure, step-by-step building instructions (digital PDF), and a custom gift box. You can also add a display stand and nameplate as optional extras.
Absolutely! After receiving your digital preview, you get up to 2 free revision rounds. We'll adjust colors, accessories, hairstyle, and other details until you're completely satisfied with the design.
Yes! We ship worldwide. Domestic orders typically arrive in 3–5 business days, while international shipping takes 7–14 business days depending on your location. All orders include tracking information.
Prices start at $49.99 for a standard custom BrickHeadz figure. Premium options with additional accessories, dual figures, or complex designs are available at higher tiers. Visit our Order section for full pricing details.
